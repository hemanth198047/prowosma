Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This policy explains what information Prowosma (“Prowosma”, “we”, “us”), operated by {{COMPANY_NAME}}, collects when you use our project and work-management service, including its optional Meeting Assistant, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. If you have questions, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

In short. We keep your account and workspace content so the service works. If you choose to connect Google Meet, we read your meeting transcripts read-only and use them only to show you transcripts, summaries, minutes and action items inside Prowosma. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or use Google user data to train AI models. You can disconnect Google and delete your transcripts at any time.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, a password hash (never the password itself) or the identity returned by a single-sign-on provider, role, and workspace membership.
  • Workspace content — projects, tasks, goals, documents, comments, attachments, settings and similar content that you or your colleagues add.
  • Meeting Assistant content (only if used) — meeting links you register, transcripts you upload or that are imported from Google Meet or Microsoft Teams, and the summaries, minutes, notes and action items generated from them.
  • Technical and security information — IP address, browser and device type, timestamps, and activity and audit logs used to keep the service secure and to show workspace administrators who changed what.
  • Billing information (if your workspace has a paid plan) — handled by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers.

By default, Meeting Assistant works only with text transcripts. If a workspace administrator turns on the optional recording feature, audio that a person records in their browser (or uploads) is processed as described in section 4A. We never join your meetings.

2. Google user data (Google Meet)

Connecting Google Meet is optional and is done by each person for their own Google account. When you connect, Prowosma asks Google for the following permissions (“scopes”) and nothing more:

What we access and store

  • From Google we receive: your account email address; for meetings you register, the conference record, the participant display names, and the transcript text with timestamps.
  • We store: encrypted access and refresh tokens (so we can import later without asking you to sign in again); the imported transcript text with speaker names and timestamps; and the summary, minutes, notes and action items generated from it.
  • We only look up meetings that you register in Prowosma. We do not read your calendar, contacts, email, Drive files or any other Google data.

How we use it

  • Only to provide the Meeting Assistant features you asked for: displaying the transcript, generating summaries, minutes, notes and action items, and—when you approve them—creating tasks and projects in your workspace.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising, to build profiles for ads, or to sell or rent to anyone.
  • We do not use Google user data to develop, improve or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Our staff do not read your Google user data unless you ask us to (for example for support), it is needed to investigate abuse or a security incident, or the law requires it.

Who it is shared with

We do not share Google user data with third parties except (a) the service providers that are necessary to run Prowosma (see section 5), including the AI provider selected by your workspace administrator to which transcript text is sent solely to generate the summary, minutes, notes and action items you request; (b) other members of your workspace, only as you choose with the meeting’s sharing settings (a meeting is private to the person who registered it by default); (c) when required by law; and (d) in a merger or acquisition, in which case the same protections will continue to apply.

Limited Use disclosure. Prowosma’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deleting Google data

  • Disconnect inside Prowosma: Integrations → Meeting Platforms → Google Meet → Disconnect my Google account. This revokes our access at Google and deletes the stored tokens.
  • Revoke from Google: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove Prowosma.
  • Delete imported transcripts: open the meeting and choose Delete transcript; the generated documents are removed on the schedule below, or on request.
  • Everything else: email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} and we will delete your account data and any remaining Google user data within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep something.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain and secure the service, including sign-in, workspaces, notifications and integrations you turn on.
  • To generate summaries, minutes, notes and action items from transcripts when you ask for them (see section 4).
  • To respond to support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.
  • To understand reliability and performance using operational metrics and error reports that do not include your content.

We do not sell personal information and we do not show advertising.

4. AI-generated content

Meeting Assistant can use an AI language model to turn a transcript into summaries, minutes, notes and action items. Your workspace administrator chooses the AI provider and supplies its key. When you request generation, the transcript text (and, for action extraction, the names of your projects) is sent to that provider over an encrypted connection solely to produce the result. The provider’s own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing, and administrators should choose provider plans and settings that do not use API content for model training. Our attendee and project directory is not sent to the AI provider. AI output can contain mistakes: every generated statement is linked to the transcript lines it came from so you can check it, and nothing is created in your workspace until a person approves it (unless an administrator enables automatic creation).

4A. Recordings (optional)

Recording is off unless a workspace administrator turns it on, and nothing is recorded unless a person chooses to record or to upload a recording. When it is used:

  • The audio is captured in the person’s own browser from the meeting tab they choose and, if they allow it, their microphone — or it is an audio file they upload. Prowosma does not join the meeting.
  • The audio is sent to Prowosma in short pieces, stored encrypted, and sent to the speech-to-text server your workspace administrator configured (this may be run by your own organization or by a third-party service) solely to turn it into text.
  • We keep the resulting text as a transcript. The audio is deleted automatically 24 hours after the recording ends (your administrator can choose anywhere from deleting it as soon as it has been turned into text up to 72 hours). The person who made the recording, or an administrator, can delete the audio at any time.
  • We do not use recordings to train AI or speech models, and we do not listen to them except where you ask us to for support, to investigate abuse or a security incident, or where the law requires.
  • The person recording must make sure everyone in the meeting knows it is being recorded and turned into text, and has agreed where the law requires. Prowosma asks them to confirm this before it starts.

7. Security

  • All traffic between your browser and Prowosma uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Google access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, with the key held outside the database, and are never written to logs or returned by our API. Prowosma refuses to store them if encryption is not configured.
  • Every record belongs to one workspace and is isolated from other workspaces; meetings are private to the person who registered them unless shared; administrators have audit logs.
  • Meeting audio, if recording is used, is encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and is only ever sent to the speech-to-text server your administrator chose.
  • Transcript text is treated as untrusted data and cannot give instructions to the AI or to the application.

No online service can be perfectly secure. If we learn of a breach affecting your personal information we will notify you as the law requires.
